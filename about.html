About Studora

We explain how to study, not just what to study.

Studora is an independent study-skills site for students of any age and the people who support them.

Schools teach subjects; they rarely teach how memory and attention work. We bring well-established research on learning into plain language and simple tools, so every study hour counts for more.

  • Evidence first. We describe techniques that are well supported by learning research, and say so when evidence is limited.
  • No shortcuts sold. We are not a tutoring company and do not sell courses.
  • Reviewed. Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.
